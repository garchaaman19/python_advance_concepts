# Isolation forest 
- Why isolation forest ? 
1. performs well with noise. 
2. unsupervised algorithm explicitly designed for anomaly detection.
3. works without labels.
3. performs well even when the data is sparse or high-dimensional due to its random feature selection


- Working of Isolation forest
1. Isolation Forest does it by introducing (an ensemble of) binary trees that recursively generates partitions by randomly selecting a feature and then randomly selecting a split value for the feature. The partitioning process will continue until it separates all the data points from the rest of the samples.
